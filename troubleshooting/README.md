# Troubleshooting

Evidence-led diagnosis and failure analysis for outdoor LCD systems.

## Planned coverage

- Overheating
- Black screen, flickering and green screen
- Backlight problems
- Power-supply failures
- Constant-current driver problems
- Condensation
- Abnormal lines and image artifacts

## Status and contribution standard

Foundation only: no technical articles are published here yet. These topics are directions, not claims about SUNTUNE products or field results.

Future contributions must distinguish **Established engineering principle**, **Engineering estimate** and **SUNTUNE-verified data** where applicable. Mark missing verification `[VERIFICATION REQUIRED]`, document sources and limitations, and obtain human approval before merging.

Separate symptoms from suspected causes and confirmed findings. Future procedures must state prerequisites, safe access limits and when to refer work to qualified service personnel.

See [contribution requirements](../CONTRIBUTING.md) and [return to the library](../README.md).
