# Field Notes

A future home for authorized observations that support the engineering pillars.

## Planned coverage

- Installation context and operating conditions
- Observed symptoms and evidence
- Interventions and outcomes
- Limitations and unresolved questions

## Status and contribution standard

Foundation only: no field reports are published here yet. These topics are directions, not claims about SUNTUNE products or field results.

Future contributions must distinguish **Established engineering principle**, **Engineering estimate** and **SUNTUNE-verified data** where applicable. Mark missing verification `[VERIFICATION REQUIRED]`, document sources and limitations, and obtain human approval before merging.

Record configuration, dates, conditions, observation method, uncertainty and authorization. Separate observations from inferred causes. Do not publish customer identities, private locations, supplier details or confidential evidence without authorization.

See [contribution requirements](../CONTRIBUTING.md) and [return to the library](../README.md).
